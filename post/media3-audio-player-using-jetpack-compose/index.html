A Step-by-Step Guide to Crafting a Custom Audio Experience in Android

Dec 03, 2024
3 minute read

For the past 8 years, I’ve tackled various challenges, but last month was all about media and playback. This article kicks off a new series called TMIL: This Month I Learned — where each month, I’ll dive into a specific topic and share my insights and experiences.

Setting the Stage

Android development has come a long way in recent years, with Jetpack Compose transforming the way we build UIs. Alongside Compose, AndroidX Media3 has replaced the older ExoPlayer API, offering a more modern and flexible solution for handling media playback. Together, they provide a powerful combination for building advanced audio and video players.

In this article, we’ll use Media3 and Compose to create a custom audio player with smooth controls and a polished design.

Let’s dive in!
